Home Health Agencies (HHAs) in the U.S. provide healthcare services to individuals in their homes, primarily for patients who are recovering from surgery, illness, or managing chronic conditions. These services can include nursing care, physical therapy, speech therapy, occupational therapy, and assistance with activities of daily living. HHAs are often utilized by elderly patients, individuals with disabilities, and those who need long-term care but prefer to remain at home instead of in a hospital or nursing facility. This dataset includes only the data of Private Equity-owned HHAs across the United States.
